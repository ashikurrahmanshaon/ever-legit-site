About Ever Legit

Ever Legit LLC is a private company that builds and operates businesses across e-commerce, international trade, software and digital marketing.

Why one company

Commerce, Software and Agency are not separate brands with separate owners. Each one is run by Ever Legit LLC, so whichever business you deal with, the same company is accountable.

How we work

  • Built to scale

    We think beyond short-term execution. Systems and processes are designed to carry growing volume.

  • Technology first

    We use software and automation to simplify and speed up business.

  • Global perspective

    Cross-border trade, distributed platforms and international customers inform our decisions.

  • Practical execution

    Ideas matter. Execution matters more. We prioritise shipping working software and operating real commerce flows.

  • Long-term thinking

    Sustainable business models and transparent partner practices guide our path.

Standards we hold

How Ever Legit conducts business across its ventures, partnerships and projects.

  • No deceptive claims or unverified proof
  • Supplier vetting before trade commitments
  • Data security and privacy safeguards
  • Tested, clean software engineering
  • Clear terms and milestone-based execution
  • Long-term value over short-term hype
